1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Mahalaxmi Telecom Private Limited (“Mahalaxmi Telecom”, “we”, “us”) is a company incorporated in India with its office at Shop No. 420, Subhash Chowk, Mangal Gate Police Chowki, Nandurbar, Maharashtra 425412. We run multi-brand mobile and electronics retail stores under the names Jagyasi Mobile, MM Mobile, Phone Café and Mobile Point, and we offer franchise opportunities.

For the personal data described in this policy, we are the “Data Fiduciary” under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: we decide why and how it is used. You are the “Data Principal”, the person the data is about.

This policy covers this website and its enquiry form only. Information we collect in our stores (for example for billing, warranty or customer finance) is handled under separate notices given at that time.

2. The laws we follow

We handle your personal data in line with:

  • the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the “DPDP Act”) and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (the “DPDP Rules”); and
  • the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, to the extent they continue to apply.

The DPDP Rules were notified on 13 November 2025 and are being phased in. The core duties on notice, consent and data principal rights become enforceable on 13 May 2027. We have chosen to follow them from now.

We collect only what is needed to deal with your enquiry. Everything in the first table is provided by you voluntarily when you fill in the enquiry form on our Contact page.

When you visit the site, the servers that host it record standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested and the date and time. We use this only to keep the website running, secure and free from misuse.

What we do not ask for

We do not ask for Aadhaar, PAN, bank or card details, passwords, health information, biometrics or any other sensitive information through this website. Please do not include such details in your message. We do not knowingly collect data from children (see section 14).

4. Why we use your personal data

We use your personal data only for these specific purposes:

  • to respond to your enquiry and contact you by phone, SMS or email about it;
  • to assess and discuss a possible franchise or business relationship with you;
  • to keep a record of your enquiry and our communications with you;
  • to protect the website and our systems against misuse, fraud and security incidents; and
  • to comply with the law, or to respond to a lawful request from a court or authority.

We will not use your details for any other purpose, including unrelated marketing, without first asking for your separate consent.

5. Consent

We rely on your consent. Before you can submit the enquiry form you must tick a box confirming that you agree to this policy and to our processing your data for the purpose of responding to your enquiry. The box is never pre-ticked, and consent is not bundled with anything unrelated to your enquiry.

Withdrawing consent. You may withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, by emailing or writing to us using the details in section 16. Once you withdraw, we will stop processing your data within a reasonable time and ask our service providers to do the same, and we will erase it unless a law requires us to keep it. Withdrawal does not affect anything we lawfully did before you withdrew. If you withdraw, we may no longer be able to continue with your enquiry.

Use without consent. The DPDP Act allows personal data to be used without consent in a few limited situations, for example to comply with a law or a court order. We will rely on these only to the extent the law permits.

6. Who we share your data with

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We do not pass your enquiry to other franchisees, store owners or mobile brands without your permission. We share it only with:

  • Service providers (Data Processors) who help us run the website and handle enquiries. Currently these are Google, whose Apps Script service and related storage and email tools receive and hold the enquiry form submissions, and the provider that hosts our website. They may use your data only on our instructions and under contractual security and confidentiality obligations.
  • Our own directors, employees and franchise team, only those who need it to handle your enquiry.
  • Professional advisers such as lawyers, auditors and accountants, under a duty of confidentiality.
  • Courts, regulators and government authorities, where the law requires or a lawful order is made.
  • A successor business, if we merge with, or sell part of our business to, another company. The successor must continue to protect your data as described in this policy.

7. Transfers outside India

Our service providers, including Google, may store or process data on servers located outside India. The DPDP Act allows transfers of personal data outside India except to countries the Central Government restricts by notification. We will comply with any such restriction and will use providers that apply appropriate security safeguards.

8. Third-party content on this website

Some content on the website is loaded from other companies:

  • Google Maps on the Contact page, which shows our store location;
  • Unsplash, from whose servers some images are delivered; and
  • Facebook and Instagram, which we link to from the website.

When these load or when you click through, your browser may send details such as your IP address and device information directly to those companies. We do not control this, and their own privacy policies apply. We do not pass your enquiry form data to them.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

As at the effective date, we do not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies on this website. The site saves a small flag in your browser’s session storage so that the opening animation is not replayed on every page. It contains no personal data and is cleared when you close the browser tab.

Third-party content such as the embedded map may set its own cookies, which you can block or delete in your browser settings. If we later add analytics or similar tools, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where the law requires.

10. How long we keep your data

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a business relationship: we erase them within 24 months of our last communication with you, or sooner if you ask or withdraw consent.
  • If you become a franchise partner: your data is then kept under the franchise agreement and its separate notice, and for the periods that company, tax and other laws require.
  • Security and access records: certain logs may be kept for at least one year, as the DPDP Rules require.
  • Legal claims: we may keep data for longer where needed to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim.

When the purpose has been served and no law requires us to keep the data, we erase it and ask our service providers to do the same.

11. How we protect your data

We take reasonable security safeguards to protect personal data from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. These include encrypted (HTTPS) connections to the website, restricting access to enquiry data to authorised team members, password-protected accounts for the systems that hold it, and confidentiality obligations on staff and service providers.

No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India in the manner and within the time the DPDP Act and DPDP Rules require.

12. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access information: ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you, the processing we carry out, and the other parties we have shared it with;
  • Correct, complete or update your data;
  • Erase your data, unless we need to keep it by law;
  • Withdraw consent at any time (section 5);
  • Raise a grievance with us and receive a response (section 16); and
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to do so.

How to exercise them. Email or write to us using the details in section 16 and tell us what you would like us to do. We may ask you for details to confirm that the request really comes from you, because we must not disclose or change data on a stranger’s request. We do not charge a fee to exercise these rights. We will respond within the time the law prescribes (currently not more than 90 days for grievances) and aim to respond much sooner.

13. Your responsibilities

The DPDP Act also places a few duties on you. Please give only accurate information, do not impersonate anyone or submit another person’s details without their permission, and do not file false or frivolous complaints. Breaching these duties can attract a penalty under the Act.

14. Children

This website is intended for adults. A “child” under the DPDP Act is anyone under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children, and our franchise opportunities are open only to adults who can enter into a binding contract. If you believe a child has sent us their details, please tell us and we will erase them.

15. Language
